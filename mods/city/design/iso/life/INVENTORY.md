# LIFE inventory: what moves or lives on top of the world

Source read on 2026-10-02 (branch t3code/openra-city-builder @ da8fd63d4a).

## 1. What exists today (top-down art)

### Vehicles (`sequences/vehicles.yaml`, `transit.yaml`, `services.yaml`)
All vehicles are single-frame sprites with **32 facings** (`Facings: -32`, clockwise from north), 256x128 sheets
of 32x32 cells, drawn at `SpriteScale 0.85`. No animation, no light frames.

| Image | File | Used by |
|---|---|---|
| car-a..car-d | car-?.png | `TrafficSim.Cars` (private cars, hashed per trip), also parked cars |
| truck | truck.png | trip types truck, freight, semi; `Logistics.TruckVehicleType` |
| van | traffic/van.png | van, maintenance (service VehicleType) |
| bus | bus.png | bus, metro (metro has no own sprite; it aliases bus) |
| taxi | pt/taxi.png | `TransitLayer.TaxiImage`, `Others` |
| tram | pt/tram.png | `TransitLayer.TramImage` (single body) |
| train, traincar | pt/train.png, pt/traincar.png | `TransitLayer.TrainImage/TrainCarImage` |
| garbage | traffic/garbage.png | garbage service |
| ambulance, firetruck, policecar, hearse | traffic/*.png | health, fire, police, deathcare |
| svc-heli | svc/heli.png (Tick 2, Offset 0,-24) | `ServiceEffects.HeliImage` (helicopter VehicleType) |

Service `VehicleType` values in rules: ambulance, firetruck, garbage, hearse, helicopter, maintenance, policecar, van.
Trips with unknown types fall back to a hashed passenger car.

### Movement (from `TrafficSim.Render.cs`)
- Vehicles move per cell along a **quadratic Bezier** from the entry edge midpoint through the cell centre to the exit
  edge midpoint; facing = tangent yaw. So straight runs are along the cell axes, turns sweep through the diagonals.
- Right-hand traffic. Lane offset from the road centre in 1/1024 cell: `LaneOffset 190` for one lane per direction;
  multi-lane `40 + (2*lane+1)*220/lanes` (2 lanes: 150 and 370).
  On the 64x32 tile a cell edge is ~35.8 px, so offset 190 = **~6.6 px** from the centre line.
- Queues: `SlotsPerLane 5`; trucks/buses/trams take 2 slots, fire engine 2. Car spacing ~1/5 cell (~7 px along axis).
- Wrecks: car sprite rotated +60 deg, grey tint (0.7, 0.7, 0.75).
- Pedestrians: image `pedestrian`, 8 frames = 4 colours x 2 steps, no facings, drawn at 0.7 scale,
  450/1024 cell to the right of the walking direction (sidewalk). Cap 200.

### Effects
- `svc-fire`: building flames, animated (Tick 90). `svc-pile`: garbage heaps small/medium/large.
- `envlights` (additive): window glows 0..3, street lamp pool 4, headlight 5, cool office glow 6, furnace glow 7.
- `envsnow`: 4 translucent snow cell variants. `envflood`: 2 flood frames.
- `CityWeatherFx`: rain and snow are **procedural particles** (streaks/dots), lightning flash. No sprites.
- `CityAtmosphere`: day/night/season/overcast are a **global tint** (post-process), no sprites.
- `PollutionLayer`: ground, air, noise, water maps; only shown in info-view heat overlays. No world-space FX.
- No chimney smoke, steam, dust, sparks, fireworks, puddles, cloud shadows exist.

### Markers
- `envicons`: 8 severity tiles (20x20, CS2 colours grey/blue/yellow/orange/red/dark red/black/green) + 24 glyphs (16x16),
  one per `CityProblem`: NoPower, NoWater, NoSewage, DirtyWater, NoRoad, Abandoned, Unhappy, NoWorkers, NoCustomers,
  NoGoods, Garbage, Fire, Crime, Sick, Ambulance, HighRent, Traffic, AirPollution, GroundPollution, Noise,
  LeveledUp, Collapsed, NoService, Flooded. Drawn by `WithCityStatusIcons` above the roof (2 icons max, cycling).
- `statusicons` (6 frames, 16x16): legacy.
- `overlays`: zone (7), heat (11), valid, invalid, grid (top-down cell tiles).

### Cursors (`cursors.yaml`, 1x, 256x192 sheet generated by tools/genui.py)
default, select, generic-blocked, move, move-blocked, sell, sell-blocked, city-road, city-zone, city-bulldoze,
city-place, city-blocked, joystick-all, scroll-{8 dirs}(+blocked), joystick-{8 dirs}(+blocked).

## 2. What a CS2-style city needs (LIFE design targets)

### Vehicles (8 directions each: N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW in world terms)
- Private cars: hatchback, sedan, estate, SUV, van (MPV), pickup, sports car; 8 body colours each.
- Taxi; bus, articulated bus; tram (2-3 segments: cab, middle, cab); train loco + passenger coach + freight
  wagons (box, tank, hopper, flat/container); metro (cab + car, visible at elevated/portal sections).
- Trucks: delivery van, box truck, articulated lorry (tractor + trailer), tanker, timber, ore/tipper, farm tractor.
- Services: garbage truck, fire engine, police car, ambulance, hearse, postal van, maintenance pickup, snowplough.
- Air/water: medevac helicopter, fire helicopter (rotor frames), cargo plane (prop frames), cargo ship, ferry/boat.
- Night: headlight + tail-light overlays per direction; siren frames for police/fire/ambulance.
- Wreck variants (crashed car).

### People
- Walkers: child, adult, elder; varied shirt/trouser/skin/hair; 4 diagonal directions (iso sidewalks run
  along cell axes, which are the screen diagonals) x 4-frame walk cycle.
- Activities: sitting (bench), waiting at stop, cycling, jogging, umbrella in rain, worker with hard hat.
- Animals: dog, birds (pigeons pecking, flying).

### FX
- Smoke white + dark (rising, 8 frames), steam puffs, fire stages (small, medium, large, burnt smoulder),
  water spray (fire hose), construction dust, sparks, fireworks.
- Night: street-light pool, window glow, headlight cones, tail-light glow.
- Weather: rain streaks + splashes, snow flakes, fog bank tile, puddles, cloud shadow.
- Pollution: smog haze tile, ground stain, noise rings. Flood water tile (animated).

### Markers and cursors
- RCT2 thought-bubble status icons for all 24 problems + severity rims.
- Selection / hover diamond brackets for 1x1 .. 3x3 footprints, vehicle selection ring.
- Placement ghost tint (valid green, invalid red) footprints.
- Route lines (bus, tram, train, metro colours), vehicle-follow path dots, waypoint flags.
- Cursors: pointer, hand (grab/pan), build, road, zone brush, bulldoze, inspect, move, blocked, place,
  scroll arrows (8), plus blocked variants.
